### What I do

I've been running **Two Monks Marketing (TMM)** since 2019 — a full-service performance marketing agency helping D2C brands and service businesses get leads and sales that actually show up in the bank account.

- 📈 **Paid media** — Meta Ads, Google Ads (Search, PMax, YouTube), Amazon Ads
- 🎯 **Lead gen & D2C sales** — funnels, CTWA / WhatsApp campaigns, landing pages built to convert
- 🔎 **SEO & AI search** — audits, content plans, and building out our AI SEO / GEO vertical
- 🎬 **Creative** — ad creatives, UGC, Reel edits, video production
- 🛍️ **Web** — Shopify stores and custom websites
- 🧭 **Strategy** — channel plans, KPI proposals, monthly reporting

We work with brands across **India, the UAE and Canada** — jewellery, fashion & apparel, real estate, healthcare, education, home décor and SaaS.
